[size=5][b]Cargo Elevator[/b][/size]

Move items, colonists and animals between the levels of one location in RimWorld 1.6: the surface, DeepRim layers and MultiFloors levels.

Cargo Elevator brings conveyor-style automation to multi-level colonies. Items put into an elevator on one level come out at its partner on another level, into storage there (or on the floor), without a pawn carrying them across. Elevators can connect to VFE conveyor belts.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Two sizes[/b], linked in pairs: a 1x1 cargo elevator and a 2x2 large one, four times as fast.
[*][b]Storage on the sending side:[/b] colonists haul into it like into a shelf, and conveyor belts can feed it.
[*][b]Storage on the receiving side:[/b] items go into stockpiles, shelves or hoppers next to the partner, or onto the floor nearby if you allow it.
[*][b]Transport loader:[/b] choose items, people and animals in the familiar portal window and send them through the elevator.
[*][b]Per-elevator options:[/b] buffer size, first in first out, merge stacks, drop on floor, allow insert.
[*][b]Status at a glance:[/b] green or red lamp, a status line with the reason for any pause, and a soft glow for dark underground rooms.
[*][b]Power at both ends:[/b] more while moving items, little while idle.
[*][b]Mod settings:[/b] speed, buffer size and power for each size, the lights, and verbose logging.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Basic usage[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Research [b]cargo elevators[/b] (after Electricity). The buildings are in their own Cargo elevator tab, or in Structure > Elevation with Better Architect Menu.
[*]Build an elevator, then one of the same size on another level at the same position measured from the map centre. They link by themselves; use "Connect to..." if several match.
[*]Power both ends.
[*]Set the sending elevator's storage filter (nothing is allowed by default). Colonists or conveyors put items in; they arrive at the partner and go into storage next to it.
[*]To send a specific amount of items, or people and animals, build a transport loader next to an elevator and use "Open transport menu".
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Required:[/b] Harmony.
[*][b]Optional, in any combination:[/b] DeepRim, MultiFloors, Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory (conveyors), Better Architect Menu.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Download the file and extract it.
[*]Copy the [b]CargoElevator[/b] folder (the one that contains About and Defs) into your RimWorld [b]Mods[/b] folder.
[*]Install Harmony the same way if you don't have it.
[*]Start the game, open Mods, enable Harmony and Cargo Elevator, and restart when asked.
[/list]
Mod load order: load Cargo Elevator after Harmony, DeepRim and MultiFloors.
